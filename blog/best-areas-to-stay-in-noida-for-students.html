Best Areas to Stay in Noida for Students (2026 Guide)

By AMY HOUSE PG · Updated 17 November 2026 · 5 min read

Noida is one of the most popular student cities near Delhi, with big universities, coaching centres and IT offices spread across many sectors. Where you stay matters more than most students expect: a PG close to your college saves hours every week and money on travel. This guide explains how to choose an area, and what to look for in each of the main student zones.

What to check before choosing an area

  • Distance to your college or coaching centre. Aim for walking distance or a short auto ride. A long commute in Noida traffic adds up quickly.
  • Metro or bus access. Good public transport makes weekends and trips home easier.
  • Food options. A PG with meals included, or cheap, clean eateries nearby, saves both time and money.
  • Safety and lighting. Visit once in the evening. Check CCTV, security guards and how the area feels after dark.
  • Total monthly cost. Compare rent plus food, travel and extras, not rent alone.

Sector 125, 126 and 127 (near Amity University)

This belt along the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway is popular with students of Amity University and nearby institutes. The main advantage is being able to walk to campus. Many PGs here include meals and basic services, because the area has fewer everyday restaurants than older sectors.

Good for: students who want a short walk to campus. Keep in mind: check that the PG provides food and has reliable power backup.

Sector 62 (IT offices and colleges)

Sector 62 is a busy institutional and office area with several colleges and many companies, and it is connected by the Blue Line metro. It suits students and interns who want access to offices and a lively market area.

Good for: students and interns who rely on the metro. Keep in mind: demand is high, so good rooms fill up fast.

Sector 15, 16 and 18 (central Noida)

These central sectors have the metro, large markets, malls, restaurants and hospitals close by. They are convenient for daily needs and are well connected to Delhi.

Good for: students who value markets, food and metro access. Keep in mind: the area is busier and can cost more than quieter sectors.

Greater Noida (Knowledge Park)

Greater Noida is home to several universities and has more open, quieter surroundings. It can be a good choice if your college is there, but travel to central Noida or Delhi takes longer.

A checklist for visiting a PG

  • Ask exactly what the rent includes: meals, WiFi, electricity, laundry.
  • Check the room for AC, wardrobe, study desk and an attached bathroom.
  • Look at the kitchen or mess and taste the food if you can.
  • Ask about security, CCTV, visitor rules and emergency help.
  • Get the rent, deposit and notice terms in writing.
